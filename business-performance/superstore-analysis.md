# **Superstore Profit Decline Analysis (Power BI + Tableau)**  
**Author:** Nasif Abbas — Data Analyst  
**Location:** Riyadh, Saudi Arabia  
**Report Date:** July 2026  

---

## **📌 Project Overview**  
Superstore experienced a **steady decline in profit** across multiple regions despite maintaining strong sales. Using **Power BI** and **Tableau**, I conducted a full profitability investigation covering:

- Product performance  
- Regional margin trends  
- Discount behavior  
- Monthly sales & profit patterns  

The dashboards revealed **566.73K in revenue lost to discounts**, **530.89K in recoverable profit**, and clear patterns of **margin erosion** driven by lossmaking products and aggressive discounting.

This case study summarizes the key insights and business recommendations.

---

## **📊 Key KPIs**  
- **Total Sales:** 2.30M  
- **Total Profit:** 286.40K  
- **Revenue Given Away (Discounts):** 566.73K  
- **Recoverable Profit:** 530.89K  
- **Critical Loss Orders:** 22  

These KPIs highlight that the issue is **not low sales**, but **profit leakage**.

---

## **📉 1. Underperforming Subcategories**  
The dashboard shows a small group of products driving most losses.

### **Top Profit Contributors**
- Copiers — 55.62K  
- Phones — 44.52K  
- Accessories — 41.94K  
- Paper — 34.05K  
- Binders — 30.22K  

### **Lossmaking Subcategories**
- **Tables — –17.73K**  
- **Bookcases — –3.47K**  
- **Supplies — –1.19K**  
- **Fasteners — 0.95K (barely profitable)**  

**Interpretation:**  
Losses are concentrated in a few products, suggesting issues with:

- Pricing strategy  
- High cost structure  
- Excessive discounting  
- Weak demand  

These items require immediate review.

---

## **📆 2. Sales & Profit Trends Over Time**  
Sales remain stable throughout the year, peaking around **August** and **December**.

Profit, however:

- Fluctuates between **0K and 0.10M**  
- Fails to rise proportionally with sales  
- Shows months with high sales but low profit  

**Interpretation:**  
This mismatch confirms **margin erosion**, not sales decline.

---

## **💸 3. Discount Impact Analysis**  
The “Sales by Discount and Profit” scatter plot shows:

- High discounts strongly correlate with low profit  
- Several discounted transactions fall into **negative profit**  
- Even high‑sales orders become lossmaking due to discounting  

**Dashboard Values:**

- **566.73K** revenue given away  
- **530.89K** recoverable profit  
- **22** critical loss orders  

**Interpretation:**  
Discounting is the **single largest driver** of profit decline.

---

## **🌍 4. Regional Profit Comparison**  
### **Sales Share**
- West — 31.58%  
- Central — 29.55%  
- East — 21.82%  
- South — 17.05%  

### **Profit Share**
- West and East perform well  
- **South and Central show severe margin erosion**

**Interpretation:**  
The South region is the **most affected**, with declining profit despite stable sales.

---

## **🧠 5. Root Causes of Profit Decline**
Based on dashboard insights, the decline is driven by:

1. **Lossmaking subcategories**  
2. **Aggressive discounting**  
3. **Regional margin erosion**  
4. **Poor pricing discipline**  

The business is losing profit **not because of low sales**, but because of **poor margin control**.

---

## **💡 6. Recommendations**

### **1. Adjust Pricing & Discount Strategy**
- Reduce discounts on lossmaking products  
- Introduce minimum margin thresholds  
- Implement region‑specific discount controls  

### **2. Reassess Underperforming Products**
- Conduct cost analysis on Tables, Bookcases, Supplies, Fasteners  
- Consider redesigning or discontinuing lossmaking items  
- Explore alternative suppliers  

### **3. Strengthen Regional Oversight**
- Prioritize the South region  
- Train managers on pricing discipline  
- Deploy margin‑tracking dashboards  

### **4. Optimize Inventory & Operations**
- Reduce stock of lossmaking items  
- Increase focus on high‑profit products  
- Improve forecasting  

### **5. Target Recoverable Profit**
- Tighten discount rules  
- Improve deal approval workflows  
- Reduce margin‑eroding transactions
