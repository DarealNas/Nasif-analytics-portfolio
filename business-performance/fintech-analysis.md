## **📌 Project Overview**  
This FinTech Capstone was a **cross‑functional product build**, combining the expertise of:

- **Project Managers**  
- **Data Analysts**  
- **Frontend & Backend Engineers**  
- **Digital Marketers**  
- **Product Designers**

The goal was clear:

> **Identify the biggest financial problems in the dataset and build a real MVP to solve them.**

My role as the **Data Analyst** was to:

- analyze 32,424 user financial records  
- identify structural risk patterns  
- validate insights with segmentation  
- quantify severity and population impact  
- translate findings into product requirements  
- support the design and engineering teams with data‑driven logic  

The final MVP we built was named **Finguard** — a financial risk monitoring tool designed to help users avoid over‑extension and improve financial stability.

---

# **🏆 The Finguard MVP**  
Based on the dashboard findings, the team built **Finguard**, a 3‑feature MVP targeting the most severe and actionable problems:

### **1️⃣ Debt Burden Monitor**  
Flags high‑risk short‑term loans and nudges users toward safer refinancing options.

### **2️⃣ Cashflow Buffer Alerts**  
Warns users when spending approaches dangerous levels (70–85% of income).

### **3️⃣ Credit Health Insights**  
Provides guidance for users with subprime credit scores (<580).

These three features directly map to the **three biggest problems** identified in the dataset.

---

# **📊 Key Finding: Financial Risk Is Structural, Not Demographic**  
Across all segmentation (region, age, gender, education, job title), risk levels remained almost identical.

This means:

> **Finguard must be built for everyone — not a narrow persona.**

This insight shaped the entire product direction.

---

# **📈 Cluster A — Debt Burden (DTI Risk)**  
### **Headline Insight:**  
**82.2%** of employed users with active loans exceed the **0.4 DTI threshold**.

### **Why It Matters**  
This is the **sharpest and most alarming** number in the dataset.

### **Actionable Driver**  
Short-term loans (12–36 months) create severe DTI spikes.

### **Finguard Feature Built**  
**Debt Burden Monitor**  
- Flags short-term loans at origination  
- Recommends refinancing  
- Helps users avoid high monthly payments  

---

# **💸 Cluster D — Thin Cashflow Buffer**  
### **Headline Insight:**  
**33.3%** of users spend over **70%** of their income.  
**8.5%** spend over **85%**, leaving almost no buffer.

### **Why It Matters**  
This is the **broadest-reaching** problem in the dataset.

### **Finguard Feature Built**  
**Cashflow Buffer Alerts**  
- Early-warning notifications  
- Spending trend monitoring  
- Personalized budgeting nudges  

---

# **📉 Cluster B — Credit Risk**  
### **Headline Insight:**  
**50.7%** of users have credit scores below **580**.

### **Why It Matters**  
This is the **largest affected population**.

### **Finguard Feature Built**  
**Credit Health Insights**  
- Score education  
- Improvement guidance  
- Risk explanations
